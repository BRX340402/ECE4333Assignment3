Overtaking (Assignment 4)

Preset 90-degree detour (default: left): the ultrasonic sees an obstacle, the car steps out 90°, drives alongside, steps back in, finds the line again and continues. Uses preset distances, not obstacle-tail detection. Default side-step: 12 cm. Emergency stop: 5 cm. At a measured trigger distance of 10 cm, default parallel travel is 51 cm (10 + 15 + 6 + 20); the initial space check also reserves 40 cm before a corner or intersection. Distances are estimated from PWM and time.

Pass allowance = obstacle length + clearance (default 26 cm). Clearance includes the vehicle turning sweep; do not use only the 7 cm rear overhang. Adjust the obstacle length to the actual object before starting.

Initial setup: stationary obstacle, car about 15 cm wide and 22 cm long. Sensor offset is estimated at 15 cm from the drive axle; verify it on the car. Moving obstacles need a separate clearance assessment.
