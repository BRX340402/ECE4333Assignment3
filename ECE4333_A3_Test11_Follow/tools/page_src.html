Stop on red 遇红停车

—Red pixels now 当前红色像素
—Threshold RP 阈值
—Traffic state 状态

ON: while following the line, when the red region reaches RP pixels the car stops and the camera checks for a STOP sign. STOP sign (2 AI hits) → stand 3 s, then go on. Not a STOP sign (red light) → wait for 2 green frames. After driving on, the same red is ignored until it has left the view. OFF: red is ignored (pure mapping). Tune RP with the live number above: hold the red target where the car should stop and read the pixels. Changing a value stops the car and saves.

开启后：循迹时红色区域达到 RP 像素就停车，并用 AI 判断是否 STOP 牌：是 STOP 牌就停 3 秒后继续；不是（红灯）就等两帧绿灯再走。重新出发后，同一个红色目标离开视野前不会再触发。把红色目标放在希望停车的位置，看上面的实时像素数来设定 RP。修改设置会先停车再保存。

Speed 速度

Corners pivot at PWM 100. 55–70 recommended. 转角固定用 PWM 100 原地转；巡航建议 55–70。

Race options 比赛选项

Applies to Start 开始 (line following). Wait for green needs Red / green detection on (Camera tab): after Start the car stands still (state WAIT FOR GREEN) until two green frames, then drives. Laps: the car counts a lap each time it finishes B-C and turns onto C-O-D, and stops there (start point, facing D). 勾选后点 Start：车先不动，看到两帧绿灯再出发；圈数 = 每次走完 B-C 并转上 C-O-D 记一圈，到圈数就停在起点。

Target following 目标跟随

—Target seen 看到目标
—Width ‰ / stop 停止宽度
—State 状态

Uses the same camera and red/green detection settings as the Camera tab (nothing is changed). Put the coloured target in front of the car and press the button; STOP CAR stops it. Outer loop: the camera gives bearing and apparent width every 0.5 s. Inner loop: a gyro PD heading controller turns the car to the target heading, and the car slows down and stops when the target is as wide as the stop width. 摄像头设置与“摄像头”页相同。把彩色目标放在车前，点按钮；STOP CAR 停车。

PD gains 参数

If the car turns AWAY from the target tick “image mirrored”. If it under-turns raise the field of view, if it overshoots lower it; if it wobbles raise Kd or lower Kp. 车背向目标时勾选“图像镜像”；转不够就加大视角，转过头就减小；摆动就加大 Kd 或减小 Kp。

Manual movement 手动驾驶

Hold an arrow to move; release to stop. 按住箭头移动，松开停车。

Line sensors 地面传感器

Waiting for floor sensors…

Stop the car. Put ALL three sensors over each surface in turn at normal ride height. Each sample takes 6 fresh readings. The UNO adds ±40 hysteresis around the threshold.

Gyroscope 陀螺仪

Turning the car LEFT must make the yaw on the Drive tab INCREASE. Your car (GY-521 chip up): Positive raw Z = left. 车向左转时“行驶”页的航向应增大；你的车应选 Positive raw Z = left。
